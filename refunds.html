Refund & Cancellation Policy

General Handyman Solutions · Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Booking deposits

Services are reserved with a deposit: orders of $300 or less are booked with a flat $50 deposit, and orders over $300 are booked with 40% down. The remaining balance is due when the work is completed. Deposits are charged through Stripe to your card on file for the order.

2. Cancelling before dispatch

If you cancel before a technician has been dispatched to your job, you receive a full refund of everything you have paid for that order.

3. Cancelling after dispatch

Once a technician has been dispatched, $75 is non-refundable and the remainder of what you paid is refunded.

4. Memberships

Memberships (HomeCare, Yard Care and HomeCare Plus) are prepaid and non-refundable. You can cancel any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for. Unused yard visits never roll over to the next month and are never refunded.

5. How refunds are issued

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Once we issue a refund, your bank or card issuer decides how long it takes to appear on your statement — typically a few business days.

6. Workmanship guarantee

Our workmanship is guaranteed for 30 days. If something we installed or repaired fails because of how the work was done, tell us within 30 days and we will make it right.
