Worker job board

    
The system for technicians: create your account, get reviewed once, and get paid automatically. The Team sends offers straight to specific technicians and posts the rest to the board — pick the jobs you want, and passing is always OK.

    

      
1
The offer lands
The Team posts jobs to the board with the pay shown up front — or sends one directly to you. For example “TV mount • Fairfield • Tomorrow • pays $100”. No bidding, no chasing leads.

      
2
You pick it — or pass
Accept and the job is yours; pass and it goes back to the board for the next technician. On board jobs, first pick wins.

      
3
Details unlock in the app
Once the Team confirms, the address and time unlock inside your app — you never need the customer’s phone; everything runs through the Team. Tap “On my way” and the customer follows you on the live map.
