General Handyman Solutions — Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Our services.

General Handyman Solutions ("GHS", "we", "the Team") provides handyman, home-care membership, and yard-care services booked through generalhandymans.app and our app. Services are performed by GHS technicians — independent contractors coordinated by the Team.

2. Labor only — you buy materials.

Unless we agree otherwise in writing, every price is labor only. You buy all materials and parts for your job. If a technician picks up materials on your behalf, that is arranged and priced separately before purchase.

3. Estimates.

Photo estimates are free. An in-person estimate costs $75 for non-members: when you book the visit, we place an authorization hold (not a charge) for that amount, and the fee is credited toward your repair when you approve the work the same day. The estimate fee is not a job deposit — job deposits work as described in clause 4. Members: your first in-person estimate is FREE; after that, member estimates are $50.

4. How payment works — deposit + balance.

Every job is booked with a deposit: $50 flat on jobs up to $300, and 40% of the total on jobs over $300 (for members, the tier is judged on your discounted total). The balance is due when the work is done — pay it right here in the app or on the same link the Team sent you. Memberships are subscriptions, not deposits, and are charged in full each month. Cancelling: if you cancel before a technician is dispatched, we refund everything you paid; once a technician has been dispatched, $75 is non-refundable and the rest is refunded to your card. You can follow your payment status live in the app at all times.

5. Memberships.

HomeCare $29/mo, Yard Care $75/mo (1 visit) or $150/mo (2 visits), HomeCare Plus $100/mo (1 yard visit) or $175/mo (2 yard visits) — one total monthly charge per plan, renewing monthly until cancelled. HomeCare and Plus members receive 20% off labor on jobs under $1,000 — on every menu service, including junk removal, hauling / green-waste loads and flat services — and priority scheduling. Members of every plan get one free first in-person estimate (then $50).

6. Changing or cancelling a membership.

You can change plans anytime from your account; Stripe prorates the difference (you may see a prorated charge or credit). You can cancel anytime from your account or by contacting the Team; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month — your plan stays active until then and you will not be charged again. Membership payments are charged in advance and are not refunded.

7. Yard visits.

A standard visit includes mowing, edging, blowing, and light weeding of a standard residential yard. Your first scheduled visit includes the normal yard cut. Work beyond normal maintenance — extreme overgrowth, accumulated debris or junk, hauling — is quoted separately before we do it. Unused visits do not roll over to the next month and are not refunded.

8. Scheduling.

You propose dates and times; the Team confirms every appointment — nothing is final until we confirm it. Please provide safe access to the work area (gates, pets, parking) in your home notes. If you need to move a visit, message the Team and we will confirm a new time — visits are prepaid with your membership and are not refunded.

9. ASAP / Emergency.

Urgent handyman jobs carry a +25% urgency charge, always shown to you before you book. (Yard visits are scheduled and are not subject to the urgency charge.)

10. Communication goes through the Team.

All communication between customers and technicians runs through the GHS Team. We never share your phone number with a technician, and never share a technician's number with you. Messages about your job inside the app go to the Team, and the Team coordinates everything.

11. Live tracking.

When your technician is on the way, the app may show their live location on a map only during that job and only so you can follow the arrival. Technicians share location only while en route to an active job.

12. Workmanship guarantee.

If something we did fails within 30 days of the completed job, tell the Team and we will make it right.

13. Changes to these terms.

We may update these terms; the current version always lives in the app and at generalhandymans.app. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the new terms.

14. Contact.

General Handyman Solutions — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect.

When you use General Handyman Solutions ("GHS", "we", "the Team"), we collect: account information — your name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed password (we never store your plain password); your service address and home details you provide (ZIP, home type, yard size, access notes); service requests and jobs — descriptions, scheduling details, messages with the Team, and photos you send or that document the work, including yard-visit before/after photos; and payment records — amounts, dates, and payment status. Card payments are processed by Stripe: we never see or store your full card number.

2. How we use it.

We use your information only to run the service: creating and managing your account and membership, scheduling and performing your services, processing payments and sending receipts, communicating with you about your requests, jobs, and visits, showing the live arrival map during a job, and keeping the records the law requires for taxes and accounting.

3. Live location — only during the trip.

When a technician taps "On my way" to your job, the app shares their location so you can watch the arrival on the map. Only the single latest point is kept — never a route history — and it is deleted as soon as the trip status changes. Technicians share location only while en route to an active job, and customers never receive a technician's phone number (see section 5).

4. Who processes data for us.

We use a small number of service providers, only for the jobs described here: Stripe processes card payments and memberships; Resend delivers our emails (estimates, receipts, notifications); OpenStreetMap / Nominatim converts service addresses into map coordinates for scheduling and the live map; Supabase hosts our database (accounts, requests, jobs, invoices and yard visits) and Render hosts the app itself. These providers process data on our instructions and for no other purpose.

5. What we never do.

We never sell your personal data. We never share a customer's phone number with a technician, and never share a technician's phone number with a customer — all communication runs through the Team. A technician receives a job's address and time only for a specific job assigned to them. We do not share your information with advertisers.

6. Your choices.

You can see and correct your information in the app at any time (your profile, service address, and home details). We do not send marketing messages without your consent. If you believe any information we hold is wrong, contact the Team and we will fix it.

7. Delete your account.

You can delete your account yourself, any time: log in, open your account area, choose "Delete my account", and confirm by typing your account email address. Deleting your account removes your profile and login, cancels any active membership effective immediately (membership payments are prepaid and are not refunded — see the Terms & Conditions), and signs you out everywhere. Work that is still in progress must be finished, or reassigned by the Team, before the account can be deleted. Past invoices and payment records are kept in anonymized form (without your name, email, phone, or address) as required for tax and legal purposes. You can also request deletion by writing to generalhandymans@gmail.com.

8. How long we keep data.

Account information is kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information is removed and the remaining service and payment records are kept only in anonymized form, for as long as tax and legal rules require. Photos attached to completed work are kept with the anonymized service record.

9. Contact.
