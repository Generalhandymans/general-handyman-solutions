## B. HOW IT WORKS — CUSTOMERS (área "Procesos — Cliente" en la app)

1. **Create your account** (or start with a free photo estimate — no account needed).
2. **Request your estimate** — free with photos, or in person ($75 hold for non-members; members: first one FREE, then $50).
3. **Approve your price** — you see the exact total before anything happens.
4. **Authorize, don't pay** — your card hold is only captured when the job is done.
5. **The Team confirms** your day and time (you propose, we confirm).
6. **Watch your technician on the map** — live, while he's on the way to your home.
7. **Job done** — the exact amount is charged, and you can rate the work.
Questions at any step? **Message the Team** right in the app — we coordinate everything with your technician for you.

## C. HOW IT WORKS — TECHNICIANS (área "Procesos — Técnico")

1. **Apply in the app** — your account starts **On review**.
2. **The Team activates you** — until then you can't claim jobs.
3. **See open jobs with YOUR exact pay on each one** — you never see the customer's total.
4. **Claim the jobs you can do** — the moment you claim, you get the address and time.
5. **You never get the customer's phone number** — and they never get yours. Anything you need to say about a job goes **through the Team** in the app.
6. **Run your job with the status buttons** — On my way (the customer watches the map) → Arrived → Completed.
7. **Your pay** — handyman work pays **$50/hr** (short jobs pay a $50 minimum); a standard yard visit pays **$50 flat**; your pay for every job is shown before you claim it, and it never changes after you accept.
